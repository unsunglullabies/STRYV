Privacy

STRYV is read-only against your system. This page is the whole of it.

The short version

Nothing you do inside STRYV is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go. There is no account to create, no analytics embedded in the app, and no server.

Steam libraries ATLAS reads the Steam library manifests already on your disk. It does not write to them, and it does not touch your Steam account.
Cover art For Steam games only, ATLAS fetches cover images from Valve's public CDN at cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com. Those requests tell Valve's CDN which game art you asked for. Nothing else is fetched.
System telemetry Every measurement STRYV takes stays on your machine. There is no analytics and no account.
Your settings Settings and the game library are stored locally in the app data directory. Clear them from within the app.
Updates Updates are fetched from the project's GitHub Releases.

What STRYV connects to

That is the complete list. Two hosts, both for a reason you can see.

  • cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com — cover art for Steam games in your ATLAS library.
  • github.com — the release feed and update packages.

Anything else on this list would be a bug worth reporting.

If something looks wrong

STRYV should never request something it does not need. If you see traffic you cannot account for, open an issue and say what you saw.
